3. Risk Factors: Concentration risks, market risks, operational risks
4. Opportunities: Growth potential, market positioning, competitive advantages

Structure your response with clear headings and bullet points. Be specific with numbers and comparisons. Flag any uncertainties or data gaps.

Rules:
- Ground all insights in the provided documents — do not speculate beyond the data
- Quantify claims with specific numbers where available
- Highlight trends and patterns across multiple documents if present
- Distinguish between facts stated in documents and your analytical interpretation
- Be concise but thorough
